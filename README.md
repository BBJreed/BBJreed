#### 📌 [ForgeField](https://github.com/BBJreed/forgefield) — the flagship

A full construction-management platform — drawing/document control, field
execution, safety, contracts & finance, BIM-VDC, preconstruction,
commissioning — 35+ modules across 6 pillars, built solo as an exercise in
AI-native software development. AI is infrastructure here, not a chatbot
bolted on top: voice-to-structured daily logs, COI compliance extraction,
drawing OCR + semantic spatial search, and CV-based auto punch-pin
matching, all with server-side keys and graceful degradation when AI is
unavailable.

#### 📌 [SuperSim](https://github.com/BBJreed/SuperSim-Field-Leadership-AI-Operation) — AI as the evaluator, not just the assistant

Play a 5-day work week as a construction superintendent — OSHA compliance,
trade coordination, schedule/budget tradeoffs, freeform radio directives —
and get an AI-generated executive hiring evaluation of how you led. Every
AI call has a full deterministic fallback; nothing breaks without a key.

#### 📌 [FieldLogPro](https://github.com/BBJreed/fieldlogpro) — proof against a real API, not a mock

A B2B field-logging platform built around a production-grade **Procore

Sandbox: full Authorization Code flow, single-use refresh token rotation
proven safe under 5-way concurrency, per-user mutex, exponential backoff +
